02 / Our purpose

Made to be
carried forward.

A language holds more than words. It holds the stories we tell, the knowledge we share, and the ways we see the world.

IgalaHeritage brings those connections into a shared digital archive. We document words, record pronunciations, and make space for Igala history, literature, and music—so people can keep learning, using, and sharing their heritage.

Inspired by the work of IgboAPI, and shaped by the Igala community.
